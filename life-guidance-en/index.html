To you

Are you carrying these questions alone?

  • You are facing a major decision, but there is no one you can speak to openly.
  • You keep asking whether to continue, grow, or let go of your business.
  • A chapter of caring for others has ended, and you are unsure what comes next for you.
  • Relationships with family, partners, or your team have left you quietly exhausted.
  • Worries about money seem to narrow the choices you could be making.
  • "It doesn't feel like the right time" — and time keeps passing.

Feeling uncertain is not a sign of weakness.

Those who have carried and chosen much in life are often the most careful about the next step.

What you need is not someone else's answer, but a clear sense of how to choose for yourself.

Philosophy

Not an answer handed to you,
but the clarity to choose.

Many kinds of advice simply tell you what to do.

But a future decided by someone else often leaves you searching for the next answer when things do not go as planned.

Amy Life Guidance is private, intuitive guidance that helps you gain clarity about where you are now and choose what comes next for yourself. Amy carefully reads your situation, your nature, and the flow of events, so that you can make choices you truly feel good about.

The answer is always within you. Amy's role is to offer a map and a light to help you find it.

Five Themes

Five themes of Amy's guidance

Moments of transition rarely involve only one part of life. Looking from five perspectives, Amy helps you find what you most want to focus on now.

  1. 01Life

    Life

    How you want to live from here, changing roles, and the center of the next chapter of your life.

    "I want to pause and ask whether this is the life I want."

  2. 02Work

    Work

    The direction of your business, growth or focus, independence or a new path — clarified from a leader's perspective.

    "Should I expand my business, or narrow my focus?"

  3. 03Relationships

    Relationships

    Partners, family, staff, and clients — finding a comfortable distance and a healthy way to relate.

    "I always feel on guard with my team."

  4. 04Money

    Money

    Your relationship with money, value and pricing, and your priorities — untangling what lies behind the worry.

    "I find it hard to put a confident price on my work."

  5. 05Timing

    Timing

    When to move, when to wait, and when to decide — reading the flow to choose a "when" that feels right.

    "Is now the time to relocate, launch, or sign — or should I wait?"

* A single session can cover several themes.

Q & A

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of guidance is this?

Amy Life Guidance is private, intuitive guidance. Amy does not declare your future or tell you what you must do. Through intuitive guidance across life, work, relationships, money, and timing, Amy helps you gain clarity about your current situation and organize your choices — so you can decide your next step yourself.

Are sessions available in English?

Yes. Please add our official LINE and send "English Session." We will arrange your session in English.

How are sessions held?

Sessions are held online by video call. Please join from a quiet place where you feel comfortable. Audio-only is also possible.

I'm not sure what to talk about.

That is completely fine. We will sort things out together, through a short pre-session hearing and conversation during the session.

Which should I choose: a private session or membership?

A private session suits you if you want clarity on where you stand now. Membership suits you if you would like a place to return to regularly. If you are unsure, we recommend the Standard plan, which includes a monthly question and themed guidance content.

Can I reschedule or cancel a private session?

You may reschedule once free of charge if you contact us at least two days before your session. Cancellation fees are as follows.

  • Before scheduling / up to 2 days beforeFree (full refund)
  • The day before50% of the fee
  • On the day100% of the fee
  • No-show100% of the fee
How do membership payment and cancellation work?

Membership is paid by credit card via Square and billed automatically each month. There is no minimum term. To cancel, please contact us via the official LINE at least 7 days before your next billing date. Requests after that date take effect after the next billing. There are no prorated or partial refunds.

Do unused monthly benefits carry over?

Monthly benefits are for use within that month and, as a rule, do not carry over to the next month.

Can I join VIP right away?

VIP is offered to a small number of members. Before joining, we recommend checking availability by sending "English Session" on LINE.

Will my information be kept confidential?

Yes. Everything you share, including personal and business matters, is handled with care and is never disclosed to third parties.

Can I get medical, legal, or investment advice?

Amy can help you organize your thoughts on these topics, but does not provide medical diagnosis, legal advice, tax advice, or investment advice. Please consult a qualified professional for those matters.
